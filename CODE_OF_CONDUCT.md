# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous environment for everyone participating in VORAX, regardless of experience or background.

## Expected behaviour

- Debate technical claims directly, respectfully, and with evidence.
- Critique code, methods, tests, and architecture—not people.
- Be precise about what is implemented, verified, proposed, or unknown.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, credentials, and intellectual property.
- Give actionable feedback and document reproducible failures.
- Credit original work and identify third-party dependencies and licences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, credential theft, malicious code, and knowingly falsified test or verification evidence are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation where necessary to protect the project. Decisions should be proportionate and based on observable conduct.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to repository maintainers through GitHub's private reporting/contact mechanisms. Do not publish credentials, private data, or exploit details in public issues.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository code, issues, pull requests, discussions, CI proposals, and other spaces where participants represent VORAX.
